
KT01 — Basic Algebra   [33 marks]
Section A: Sets
15. Let P = {a, b, c, d, e} and Q = {c, d, e, f, g}. Find:  (4)
a) P ∪ Q
b) P ∩ Q
c) P − Q
d) |Q|

16. Given X = {1, 3, 5, 7, 9} and Y = {1, 3}, is Y ⊆ X? Justify your answer.  (2)
17. In a class of 40 students,  
5 play soccer, 18 play basketball, and 10 play both. Using set notation, find how many students play soccer or basketball.  (3)

Section D: Basic Computing

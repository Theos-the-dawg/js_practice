
# KT01 — Basic Algebra   [33 marks]

### Section A: Sets

15. Let P = {2, 4, 6, 8, 10} and Q = {6, 8, 10, 12, 14}. Find:  (4)
    a) P ∪ Q
    b) P ∩ Q
    c) P − Q
    d) |P|
16. Given X = {2, 4, 6, 8, 10} and Y = {4, 8}, is Y ⊆ X? Justify your answer.  (2)
17. In a class of 35 students, 20 play chess, 15 play checkers, and 6 play both. Using set notation, find how many students play chess or checkers.  (3)

### Section B: Arithmetic Sequences & Series

18. Find the 10th term of the arithmetic sequence: 7, 12, 17, 22, …  (2)
19. The first term of an arithmetic sequence is −5 and the common difference is 4. Find the 15th term.  (2)
20. Find the sum of the first 20 terms of the arithmetic sequence 3, 7, 11, 15, …  (3)
21. An arithmetic sequence has a₁ = 6 and a₉ = 54. Find the common difference d.  (2)

### Section C: Geometric Sequences & Series

22. Find the 6th term of the geometric sequence: 2, 6, 18, 54, …  (2)
23. A geometric sequence has a = 8 and r = 0.5. Find the 5th term.  (2)
24. Find the sum of the first 7 terms of the geometric sequence 3, 6, 12, 24, …  (3)
25. A population of insects doubles every hour, starting with 40 insects. Using the geometric nth term formula, how many insects will there be after 6 hours?  (3)

### Section D: Basic Computing

26. List the four stages of the IPOS model and give one example of each in the context of an online shopping checkout.  (4)
27. Using an example from a smart home thermostat, explain the difference between data and information.  (3)
